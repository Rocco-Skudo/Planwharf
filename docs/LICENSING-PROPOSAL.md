The maintainer may consider a separate license for distribution of the official project or a covered derivative without the applicable GPL obligations. Such an agreement can negotiate a fee, royalty, or other terms. No alternative commercial license, rate, or signed agreement is created by these notices. Simply charging money does not trigger a separate license requirement if GPL obligations are met.

Alternative licensing requires sufficient rights to the included code. Third-party licenses remain applicable. The [Individual CLA](../CLA.md), [Entity CLA](../CLA-ENTITY.md), and [contribution rules](../CONTRIBUTING.md) cover future accepted contributions and preserve the public GPL offering; they do not retroactively establish acceptance by earlier contributors.

The GPL does not grant permission to present a fork as the official Planwharf project or as endorsed by its maintainer. Independent reimplementations that are not derivatives are not restricted by this copyright license.

[Third-party notices](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) remain in force. Provider binaries, credentials, API access, and subscription entitlements are not included or relicensed by the project's GPL notice. Existing notebooks and user-authored project content do not become project contributions merely because Planwharf stores or processes them.

This decision adopts the same public GPL-3.0-only and separately negotiated alternative licensing model documented in RaceEngineer. It replaces the earlier proposed requirement for royalties on every monetized derivative; it does not change RaceEngineer or revoke rights already granted under previous releases.
